Netflix Sub Loader — Settings

External subtitles & auto sync

Find subtitles online

Each site gives you a free API key once you sign up. One key is enough to search; add more sites for more results. Keys are stored only on this computer and are only sent to their own site.

Shortcuts while watching

  • A / D (or Shift+← / →) — previous / next line
  • G / H — subtitles 0.1 s earlier / later, hold Shift for 1 s
  • Alt+Shift+S — open/close the subtitle panel

Data on this computer

Subtitles you used and their sync results are kept for the last 40 episodes, so they are ready when you come back.
